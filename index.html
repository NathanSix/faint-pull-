        Why the numbers are so small
        
      
        
        
        m₁ ↔ m₂
        Everything pulls
        Your mug pulls on you, and you pull back on it with exactly the same force.
        
        
        F ∝ m
        More mass, more pull
        A 7 kg bowling ball pulls 20 times harder than a 350 g mug at the same distance.
        
        
        F ∝ 1/r²
        Distance cuts it fast
        Twice as far means a quarter of the pull. Ten times as far, one hundredth.
        
        
        G ≈ 10⁻¹⁰
        Why you never feel it
        Room objects pull with billionths of a newton. Earth pulls with hundreds.
